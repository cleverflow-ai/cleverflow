Exception: There is no anti-crease phase in the Woollens  and Silks  programmes.\\n\\nThe door can be opened at any time during the anti-crease phase.\\n\\n- 1) If a temperature of 60 °C or higher is selected, two rinses are carried out. Subsequent water intake for the main wash is through compartment  .\\n\\n## Programme sequence\\n\\n## PowerWash\\n\\nPowerWash is a washing technique developed by Miele. It is activated in the following programmes:\\n\\n- -Cottons (with small and medium load sizes)\\n- -ECO 40-60 (with small and medium load sizes)\\n- -Minimum iron\\n- -Shirts\\n- -Automatic plus\\n- -Delicates\\n- -Cottons  (with small and medium load sizes)\\n\\n## How it works\\n\\nConventional washing techniques wash with more water than the laundry can absorb. This total volume of water needs to be heated.\\n\\nThe PowerWash washing technique only uses slightly more water than the laundry can absorb. The water which has not been absorbed by the laundry heats up the drum and the laundry and is sprayed into the laundry over and over again. This reduces energy consumption.\\n\\n## Activation\\n\\nPowerWash is activated automatically in the programmes listed above.\\n\\nPowerWash is deactivated if:\\n\\n- -The selected final spin speed is less than 600 rpm\\n- -CapDosing ( ,  ) has been selected for the main wash\\n- -The wash temperature is higher than 60 °C\\n- -Extras such as Pre-wash or Water plus have been selected\\n- -A large load is being washed in the Cottons programme\\n\\n## Special features\\n\\n## -Drenching phase\\n\\nThe washing machine will spin a few times at the beginning of the programme. During spinning, the water discharged is sprayed back into the laundry in order to achieve optimum moisture penetration.\\n\\nThe optimum water level is set at the end of the drenching phase. If necessary, the washing machine will pump water away or take in a little more fresh water.\\n\\n- -Noises during the heating phase Unusual bubbling noises can occur while the laundry and the drum are being heated.\\n\\n## Changing the programme sequence\\n\\n## Changing a programme (safety lock)\\n\\n## Cancelling a programme\\n\\nA programme, temperature, spin speed or selected options cannot be changed once the machine has been successfully started. This prevents the machine being operated unintentionally, e.g. by children.\\n\\nIf the washing machine is switched off during active programme operation, the standby function does not switch the washing machine off completely after 15 minutes. Safety functions continue to be active because there may still be water inside the drum.\\n\\n## Interrupting a programme\\n\\n-  Switch the washing machine off with the  control.\\n-  Switch the washing machine back on with the  control to continue with the same programme.\\n\\nYou can cancel a wash programme at any time after it has started.\\n\\n-  Touch the Start/Stop sensor.\\n\\nThe following will appear in the display:\\n\\n-  Cancel the programme?\\n-  Select Yes.\\n\\nThe following will appear in the display:\\n\\nProgramme cancelled .\\n\\nThe suds will be pumped away.\\n\\n-  Wait until the door unlocks.\\n-  Pull open the door.\\n-  Remove the laundry.\\n\\n## Selecting another programme\\n\\n-  Close the door.\\n-  Select the programme you want.\\n-  Add detergent to the detergent dispenser drawer, if necessary.\\n-  Touch the Start/Stop sensor.\\n\\nThe new programme will begin.\\n\\n## Favourite programmes\\n\\n## Creating favourites\\n\\nYou can save your own customised wash programmes under names of your choice.\\n\\n## Option 1\\n\\n-  The main menu must be visible in the display.
